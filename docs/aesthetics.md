# Angel Line — moodboard curation

**Haunted 1998 network, angels in the wires.**

This is a reading of the moodboard (ten Pinterest screens plus three single references), sorted into the clusters it naturally falls into, with what each one is *made of* and how the site uses it. The named styles (Metalheart, Vectorheart, angelpunk, and others) come after, as supporting influences: they're the things liked but not pinned.

Everything built from this is original. The board is a direction, never a source to copy.

---

## The thesis

The board isn't sci-fi and it isn't cyberpunk. There's almost no neon and no skyline, and nothing about it is optimistic. It's the **late-90s internet imagined as a spiritual place**: bodies plugged into machines, angels made of hardware, dolls that might be alive, ink sketches and photocopied flyers passed around at 3am. It's quiet, pale or pitch dark, melancholy, a little eerie, sometimes violent, and always elegant.

Three tensions run through all of it:
- **Sacred and technical:** halos, crosses and wings, next to cables, serial numbers and barcodes.
- **Soft and hard:** long hair, lace and porcelain skin, next to spikes, chrome, claws and armor.
- **Drawn and rendered:** a nervous pencil line on paper, next to a glossy, perfect 3D object on white.

## The eight clusters

### 1. Wired bodies — *angels in the wires*
Figures with cables growing out of the spine, the neck, the back of the head. A cyborg curled up and suspended among floating monitors. Helmets with visors and ports, a cam strapped to a schoolgirl's head, earphone wires wrapped around a doll like a cocoon. Bodies being repaired by other bodies.
- **Made of:** black cable (always more than needed, tangled, hanging in loops), white and black armor shells, red threads where wires meet flesh, glowing screens full of text.
- **Feeling:** dependency, tenderness, being *connected* or *disconnected*. The body as a terminal.
- **On the site:** the Vessel (a doll suspended in a tank, cabled at the spine), the Signal room (floating monitors and cables in a white void), the cardiac link.

### 2. Gothic linework & flash — *the ink layer*
Fine-pen and pencil drawings: angels sketched in a few nervous strokes, ornate crosses and cathedral filigree, tattoo flash (a red-ink back piece, a forearm portrait, thigh work), handwritten script ("Angel", "seen this angel?"), spiky mechanical angels drawn as pure line.
- **Made of:** black or red ink on off-white paper, hairline strokes, thorny ornament, symmetry, vertical compositions, a lot of empty paper.
- **Feeling:** devotional and obsessive, like a sketchbook kept in secret.
- **On the site:** the seraph, the Ink render mode, the Flash room (a symmetry ink tool for drawing your own gothic flash).

### 3. Haunted network print — *the flyer layer*
CD covers and promo posters with glitchy photos ("diskonnekted", the teal "chrysalis" cover, "am I fading"), a red sun behind a figure, a "warning" stamp, barcodes, catalog numbers, Japanese type set vertically, rave flyers with exploded metallic lettering, a collage with a red block and sunglasses, a meme captioned in plain white impact type.
- **Made of:** scanned-paper texture, teal and cold-blue photos, one block of red, barcodes and serials, cramped technical small print, vertical Japanese, pixel glitches and halftone.
- **Feeling:** an artifact from a scene you were almost part of.
- **On the site:** the Press room (a poster generator in this language), the barcodes and serials everywhere, the HUD's small print.

### 4. Porcelain & dolls — *the object layer*
Ball-jointed dolls in black lace and white bonnets; doll faces with metal muzzles, gas masks and exposed mechanisms; armored dolls holding swords; figurines photographed in snow, on a shelf, on denim. White resin angels with clear wings. Hair as a material: long, straight, silver or black, often hiding the face.
- **Made of:** matte porcelain skin, glass eyes, visible joints, lace, resin, small mechanical parts; soft daylight or a single harsh flash.
- **Feeling:** uncanny calm. Objects that look back.
- **On the site:** the Vessel's doll (a porcelain cyborg built in code: capsule limbs, ball joints, segmented armor, silver hair).

### 5. Biomech & thorns — *the wound layer*
Crown-of-thorns helmets, spines covered in spikes or piercings, insect-and-spider angels with too many legs, skeletal winged figures, pale sculpted bodies with red straps, a creature that is half machine and half monster.
- **Made of:** bone white, black chitin, rust and dried red, spikes in rows, ribs and vertebrae as ornament.
- **Feeling:** beauty with teeth. The holy and the anatomical pressed together.
- **On the site:** the thorned halo, spikes and vertebrae on the seraph, barbed wire, the megastructure's ribbing.

### 6. Silver & black — *the wardrobe layer*
Industrial cross piercings, eyebrow bars, ear cuffs, stacks of heavy silver rings, claw rings, spiked and chain bracelets, chrome gloves. Black high-collar military coats, lace-up leather, fur hoods, black long hair with blunt bangs, flared black pants.
- **Made of:** worn silver, oxidized metal, black leather and wool, high collars, buckles and buttons in rows.
- **Feeling:** armor as jewelry, jewelry as armor.
- **On the site:** chrome is always slightly worn and spiked rather than shiny-new; details come in rows (rivets, buttons, thorns); the UI's corner brackets and hairline frames work like hardware.

### 7. Megastructure & ruin — *the world layer*
Endless concrete and pipes with tiny figures in black gear, rooftops in fog, an angel on a gallows under a red sky, skeletal giants among towers, a blue cyborg sitting on wrecked machinery.
- **Made of:** grey concrete, rebar, fog, scale that is far too big, dark painted grit with scratched highlights.
- **Feeling:** lonely, monumental, long after the end.
- **On the site:** the main menu, a massive abstract structure the camera flies across, with each room docked at one of its far edges.

### 8. Clean white renders — *the gloss layer*
A glossy cyborg suspended among monitors on pure white; a burgundy racing mech-woman with team decals and numbers, with a white bike, a hard floor shadow and nothing else.
- **Made of:** high-key light, glossy lacquer and chrome, decals, logos, numbers printed on armor, hard shadows, empty white space.
- **Feeling:** product shot from a future that never shipped.
- **On the site:** the Signal room's white void, decals and serials on objects, the doll's lacquered armor plates.

## The language

**Palette**
| Role | Colour | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Ground (dark) | ink black `#070708` | black fashion, night photos, megastructure |
| Ground (light) | bone and paper `#ecebe6`, white void `#f3f3f1` | sketch paper, white renders |
| Metal | silver and chrome, worn | jewelry, armor, figurines |
| Accent 1 | blood red `#c3141b` | red sun, red ink tattoos, red threads in wires, gloves |
| Accent 2 | cold cyan / teal `#8fe6e8` | glitch CD covers, Miku's hair, screen glow |
| Rare | burgundy lacquer | the racing mech, dried red |

Red appears once per composition and means something: a wound, a signal, a warning. Cyan is light coming out of a screen.

**Materials:** chrome (worn, spiked), porcelain and resin, black cable, lace, ink on paper, scanned paper, CRT glass, lacquer with decals, concrete in fog.

**Type:** blackletter for names and titles (the devotional voice); wide techno capitals for labels (the hardware voice); monospace only for real data (serials, logs, readouts); vertical Japanese as texture; handwritten script used sparingly.

**Composition:** a single figure in a lot of empty space; vertical formats; small print in the margins; diagonal tension (Metalheart); HUD framing with corner brackets; one block of colour.

**Recurring symbols:** halo, wings, the cross, cables, eyes, the red sun, barcodes, serial numbers, thorns, vertebrae, monitors, dolls.

**Motion and sound:** slow. Things float, sway and breathe; glitches are short and rare. Drones, hum, a heartbeat, a click. Never upbeat.

**What is not on the board (avoid):** neon pink and purple cyberpunk, gold, rainbow gradients, cute pastel, busy skylines, emoji, generic SaaS UI.

## Supporting influences (liked, not pinned)

- **Metalheart / Depthcore** (≈1998–2004): abstract chrome forms over grids and textures, diagonal layouts, dark translucent panels with corner brackets. → menu structure, panels, HUD.
- **Vectorheart** (late 1990s–mid 2000s): thin technical lines, 45° angles, labels and diagrams. → annotations, leader lines, poster grids.
- **Angelpunk** (no published definition; read from the board): angels as hardware. → the whole lore.
- **Graphic realism** (no published definition; read from the board): glossy renders treated as graphic design. → cluster 8.
- **Alba aero** (no published definition found; closest is the Frutiger Aero family): pale glass, liquid, light. → the Vessel's water and glass.
- **Webcore and Y2K futurism:** OS windows, ASCII, badges, reticles. → ANGEL_OS and the HUD.
